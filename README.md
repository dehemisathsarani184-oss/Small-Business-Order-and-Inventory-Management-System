# Small Business Order & Inventory Management System

## Overview

This is a Business Analysis portfolio project developed to analyse and document the requirements for a Small Business Order & Inventory Management System.

The project focuses on replacing manual order and inventory management activities with a centralized system that supports better stock visibility, order tracking, customer management, and business reporting.

## Business Problem

Small businesses may manage customer orders through WhatsApp, phone calls, notebooks, and Excel spreadsheets while maintaining inventory manually.

This can lead to:

- Missed or incorrectly recorded orders
- Manual stock checking
- Inaccurate inventory records
- Difficulty tracking order status
- Decentralized customer information
- Delayed identification of low-stock products
- Manual preparation of sales information

## Proposed Solution

The proposed system provides a centralized solution for managing:

- User access
- Products
- Inventory
- Customers
- Orders
- Stock availability
- Low-stock alerts
- Order status
- Basic sales and inventory information
